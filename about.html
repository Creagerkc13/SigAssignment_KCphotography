Hi Im Karlie!

I am so happy you're here!


To tell a little about me, I am 21 years old and have been taking photos for a little over 6 years. I am currently engaged to my best friend (excited to get married soon!) and we have 2 fur babies named BB and Sparky. I am currently really into crocheting, baking, and working on home improvements.


I have always been intrested in photography, I love being able to capture raw emotion and give people memories they can keep forever. My parents bought me my first real camera at age 13 and I haven't been able to stop exploring and making my friends and family members take pictures so I can practice different techniques. I joined the yearbook squad in highschool and explored more into editing and photoshop to perfect the book we made that year.


Friends and family say that I am full of joy and laughter, and that is what I love to bring to all of my interactions with clients. I want you to feel comfortable, beautiful, and confident. I love to chat and laugh during our time together, as we create images and looks that you will treasure for a lifetime.


After you’re done looking through the website, be sure to connect with me on my Socials. I would love to chat with you!
